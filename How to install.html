OwlHUD

A talking owl companion for World of Warcraft: Forever. Version 3.4. Works for every class.

Install in five steps

  1. Unzip the whole download. Right-click the zip and choose Extract All. You should end up with a folder holding this page, Install OwlHUD and a folder called OwlHUD.
  2. Double-click “Install OwlHUD”. It finds your game and copies the OwlHUD folder into it. That is all it does.
    Windows may show “Windows protected your PC” because the file came from a friend and not a shop. Choose More info, then Run anyway.
    Rather do it by hand? Copy the OwlHUD folder into
    World of Warcraft\_forever_\Interface\AddOns\
  3. Close the game completely and start it again. A /reload is not enough the first time: the owl’s pictures only load when the game starts.
  4. On the character screen, click AddOns and check that OwlHUD is ticked.
    If it says “out of date”, tick Load out of date AddOns.
  5. Log in. After a few seconds your owl introduces himself and asks four quick things: his name, his feathers, the shape of his head, and how much he should do for you.

What to try first

Click him

Left-click opens every setting and everything you can ask him. Right-click hushes him for ten minutes.

The wardrobe

Type /owl wardrobe. He sets himself goals and earns hats, cloaks, perches and more as you play.

Rare finds

/owl lanterns and /owl torches. Every dungeon hides a lantern, some zones hide a torch, Common to Legendary.

Who he is

/owl quirks. Where he hatched, what he eats, what he fears. No two owls are the same.

Playing together

Add each other as friends

Your friends’ owls then appear beside yours whenever they are online, grouped or not, dressed the way their owners dressed them.

Same version for everyone

The owls compare versions when they meet and tell you if someone needs the update. /owl version shows yours.

Things to do

/owl dance, /owl hootoff, /owl gift, /owl post Name your note, /owl readyall, /owl pals.

Good to know

He starts as just the owl

Nothing is sold, repaired or added to your screen unless you pick “the owl and his helpers”. You can change your mind any time from his options.

He gets smarter with these

Optional addons he can read: Forever Leveling BiS for gear advice, Questie for nearby quests, GatherMate2 for gathering spots.

If something looks wrong

Type /owl report, log out, and tell Christian. A red line in chat saying a feature “turned off” is worth passing on too.

OwlHUD is a fan-made addon. All owl art is original.
