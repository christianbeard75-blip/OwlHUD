# OwlHUD

A talking owl companion for **World of Warcraft: Forever**. He sits on your screen, watches what you do, remembers it, and helps. He has a wardrobe, goals of his own, and he knows your friends' owls.

**Current version: 3.4**

## Get it

### [Download OwlHUD](https://github.com/christianbeard75-blip/OwlHUD/archive/refs/heads/main.zip)

That link always gives you the newest version.

## Install (first time, about a minute)

1. Click **Download OwlHUD** above.
2. Right-click the downloaded zip and choose **Extract All**.
3. Open the new folder and double-click **Install OwlHUD**.
4. Close World of Warcraft completely and start it again.

If Windows shows a blue warning at step 3, click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.

On the character screen, click **AddOns** and check that OwlHUD is ticked. Then log in and meet your owl. Click him, or type `/owl`.

## Update (later on, about ten seconds)

The folder you extracted also contains **Update OwlHUD**. Drag it to your desktop and keep it.

Whenever there is a new version:

1. Double-click **Update OwlHUD**.
2. In the game, type `/reload`.

That is all. It fetches the newest version from this page and puts it in the right place.

## What is new in 3.4

- **Friends' owls** sit in a tidy column beside yours, stay there whenever your friends are online, and react: they talk, cheer, slump when their player dies, and fly over to visit.
- **In a dungeon** the owls line up on one branch together, and yours remembers how long the last run took.
- **Real weather.** He reads the actual rain, snow and sandstorms, keeps notes for each zone, and starts guessing what comes next.
- **Other players.** He remembers who has killed you and who you keep beating, by name.
- **He knows you better:** your kills by type, your favourite victims, your professions, your records.
- **Quieter.** Small repeat reminders become a word over his head instead of a speech, and he no longer talks every time you go underwater.
- **Fixes:** he no longer mistakes you for one of your own friends, no longer forgets everything when the game withholds the realm name, keels over properly when you die, and never tells you to sell your fishing pole.

## Handy commands

| Type this | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| `/owl` | Everything he can do |
| `/owl weather` | The weather now and his notes for this zone |
| `/owl pvp` | Who has killed you, and who you have beaten |
| `/owl signals` | Words over his head instead of repeated reminders, on or off |
| `/owl friendowl` | Friends' owls beside yours, on or off |
| `/owl report` | Saves a report for troubleshooting |

## Something wrong?

Type `/owl report`, then `/reload`, and tell whoever sent you this page. The report says exactly what happened.
